4Get ready for the call

🏷️ Find a label

Look at your phone, headphones or clothes. Find MADE IN … and remember the country.

Найди надпись «Made in…» и запомни страну — с неё начнём созвон.

🤫 Your secret object

Choose an everyday object at home. Don't tell anyone! Prepare 3–4 clues:

  • It's made of … (plastic / metal / wood / glass)
  • It's used for … (eating / writing / listening…)
  • It's found in … (every kitchen / the bathroom / my bag)
  • B1+It was invented … (a long time ago / about 100 years ago)

Example

They're made of metal and plastic. They're used for cutting paper. They're found in every classroom.
